How the estimates work

Epley: load × (1 + reps ÷ 30). Brzycki: load × 36 ÷ (37 − reps). A completed single uses its recorded load directly.

Pull-up estimates first include bodyweight plus added weight. The added-weight view then subtracts bodyweight from the estimate.

Estimated 1RM is a rep-adjusted trend proxy, not a measured maximum. Unknown effort and reps in reserve limit precision. Each workout averages the estimates from its qualifying working sets. Whole reps within your cap qualify; fractional, kipping and unclear counts do not. Warm-ups and failed attempts are excluded from every graph. Assisted extras stay separate.

Dumbbell loads are per dumbbell. Unknown machine and band masses stay unconverted. Your entered loads and notes remain available in the session log.

Load, reps and stack settings use workout averages. Timed holds average seconds per completed hang. Volume remains a workout total of known load × base reps, including bodyweight for pull-ups. Hold totals multiply duration by hold count. These summaries include qualifying working sets only. Gaps mean gaps in this saved log, not necessarily breaks from training.
